Markham realtor directory FAQ

How are the best real estate agents in Markham ranked? +

The automated score combines Bayesian-adjusted review quality, log-scaled review volume, observed local-search visibility and profile completeness. The same formula is applied to every eligible profile.

Can an agent pay to move higher in the ranking? +

No. The editorial ranking score does not include advertising or referral payments. Any future commercial relationship will be clearly labelled and kept separate from the scoring formula.

What does Markham-based mean here? +

The data source must return a public business address within accepted Markham-area postal prefixes, including Markham, Unionville and the Markham side of Thornhill. It refers to the public business location, not necessarily the brokerage head office.

Are ratings and review counts guaranteed to be accurate? +

No. Public business data can change between refreshes or contain errors. Treat ratings as one comparison signal and verify current information directly before making a hiring decision.

How often is the directory refreshed? +

The data pipeline runs every Sunday at 00:00 UTC. A new deployment occurs only when the underlying directory data materially changes.
